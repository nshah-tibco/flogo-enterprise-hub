INDUSTRIAL GAS - CYLINDER VALVE INSPECTION CHECKLIST
Document ID: IG-CHK-VLV-031   Revision: 1   Effective date: 2026-10-01
Applies to: Houston Yard A, Houston Yard B, Dallas Distribution Center
NOTE: FICTIONAL DOCUMENT CREATED FOR DEMO PURPOSES ONLY. NOT FOR OPERATIONAL USE.

1. Purpose. This checklist defines the valve inspection every gas cylinder must pass before it is filled or dispatched.

2. Leak test interval. Every cylinder valve must be leak-tested at least every 90 days using a 1% leak-detection solution, and again after any valve repair. A valve that shows bubbles for more than 5 seconds fails the test.

3. Valve O-rings. Valve O-rings must be replaced every 24 months, or immediately if they are cracked, flattened or contaminated with oil. Oxygen cylinder valves must only use oxygen-compatible, oil-free O-rings.

4. Valve protection cap. The valve protection cap must be fitted whenever the cylinder is not connected to equipment. A cylinder found without its cap during a yard audit is tagged "HOLD" for 24 hours and re-inspected.

5. Inspector sign-off. Each inspection is recorded with the inspector code (for example VLV-7) and the date in the Smart Cylinder Operations system. A cylinder without a valve inspection in the last 90 days must not be dispatched.

6. Failed inspection. A cylinder that fails any check is set to status "Quarantine", its valve is replaced by the maintenance team, and it is leak-tested again before returning to "Available".
